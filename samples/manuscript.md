The Millhaven Lighthouse Chapters

Day 1. Professor Marta Halloran uncovered her instrument kit, and her eyes were green in the lamplight. She turned toward Millhaven harbor.

Day 2. On Day 2, Captain Elena Vance arrived at Millhaven ahead of her convoy. She tightened her grip on the rail. The keeper of the old lighthouse keeper did not see her come.

Day 3. On Day 3, Elena and Marta met at the sea wall. The old lighthouse keeper's dog barked once, twice. Marta, the Doctor, leaned closer over the maps. Elena felt the cold rising off the water.

Day 6. On Day 6, Elena fought the storm surge, her eyes were grey and flat against the spray. She drove herself across the wet rocks. The keeper of the old lighthouse keeper watched from the tower.

Day 7. On Day 7, Elena spoke with Kell about the raid at the north gate. Kell carried the rope, and Kell's eyes were grey as sea glass. On Day 7, Elena paced the deck of the ferry.

Day 8. Marta wrote her report in the tower library. The keeper of the old lighthouse keeper slept through it all. Elena felt the pull of home in her chest.
